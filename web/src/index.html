3D Pinball Space Cadet

Add the table files

Space Cadet needs its original data: PINBALL.DAT and the sounds from 3D Pinball for Windows, or CADET.DAT from Full Tilt! Pinball.

They stay in this browser after the first import.

Where to find them

Windows XP: C:\Program Files\Windows NT\Pinball

Full Tilt! Pinball: the CADET folder on the CD

Running the container? Put them in the folder mounted at /data and reload.

Or drop the folder anywhere on this page.
